# Client Contributor Guide — OtoCPA

For **contributors** — client-side team members who upload documents
but don't manage the team. If you're an admin, see
[client_admin_guide_en.md](client_admin_guide_en.md).

## 1. Accepting your invitation

1. You get an email with subject "invited you to submit receipts on
   OtoCPA".
2. Click **Accept invitation** (expires in 14 days).
3. The page is bilingual — toggle FR / EN top-right.
4. You're redirected to your **personal link**.

## 2. Your personal link

- **Unique to you.** Don't share it with anyone.
- Your CPA sees your name on every upload.
- If you lose your link, ask your team admin or your CPA to
  regenerate it.

## 3. Uploading receipts

1. Tab **Upload**.
2. Three options:
   - **Photo** (phone camera) — ideal for a single receipt.
   - **PDF** — for invoices and statements received by email.
   - **WhatsApp** — send the photo to the WhatsApp number shown.
3. Add an optional **note** (e.g., "grocery invoice").
4. Click **Send**.

You'll see a confirmation: "Thanks [your name]! N document(s)
queued."

## 4. Your upload history

Tab **My documents**:

- **Queued** — received, in processing.
- **In review** — being examined by your CPA.
- **Posted** — recorded.
- **Rejected** — returned with an explanation; action required.

## 5. Messaging

Tab **Messages** — two-way thread with your CPA.

- Ask a question about a rejected document.
- Ask how to categorize an ambiguous receipt.
- Report a missing document.

## FAQ

**Q: My upload won't go through.**
A: Check size (max 25 MB) and format (JPG, PNG, HEIC, PDF). If it
persists, message your CPA via the **Messages** tab.

**Q: I uploaded the same receipt twice.**
A: Mention it to your CPA via **Messages**; they'll remove the
duplicate.

**Q: I can't remember how something works.**
A: Guided tour: click the help icon top-right.

## Getting help

- **Your team admin** (first line).
- **Your CPA** via **Messages** tab.
- **OtoCPA support**: support@otocpa.com
