# CPA Employee Guide — OtoCPA

For employees of a CPA firm (reviewer, bookkeeper, junior) who don't
have the owner role. If you're an owner, see
[cpa_owner_guide_en.md](cpa_owner_guide_en.md).

## 1. First login + 2FA

1. Receive the invitation from your firm owner.
2. Click **Set up my account** and create your password (minimum 10
   characters, 1 digit, 1 letter).
3. **Enable two-factor authentication**: add OtoCPA to your
   authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, 1Password) and
   enter the 6-digit code.
4. Save your **backup codes** somewhere safe.

## 2. My Tasks

The **Home** page shows:

- The **shared queue**: all pending documents for the firm.
- **My Tasks**: documents assigned specifically to you by the
  owner or an admin.
- **Filters**: client, status, period, uploader, date.

## 3. Reviewing a document

Click a document to open the detail view:

1. **Image**: zoom, rotate, select region to re-OCR.
2. **Extracted fields**: date, vendor, amount, GST, QST, category.
   Correct in place if needed.
3. **Client notes**: comment left at upload time.
4. **Action**: Approve, Reject, or Escalate.

## 4. Submitting for review

If you're a junior bookkeeper:

1. Fill in the extracted fields.
2. Click **Submit for review**.
3. A senior reviewer will pick it up from their queue.

## 5. Escalating to a senior

Hit a complex case? Click **Escalate** and add a note explaining the
question. The document moves to the senior reviewers' queue with
your note attached.

## 6. Workflow tools

- **Bulk approve**: header checkbox.
- **Global search**: magnifier at the top.
- **Keyboard shortcuts**: `?` shows the full list.
- **Offline mode**: in-progress reviews save locally and sync when
  connectivity returns.

## FAQ

**Q: I lost my 2FA phone.**
A: Use a backup code OR ask your owner to reset your 2FA.

**Q: I see a document from another client by mistake?**
A: Flag it to your owner right away. Scope is per-firm; an
out-of-firm document should never appear.

## Getting help

Talk to your firm owner, or email support@otocpa.com.
